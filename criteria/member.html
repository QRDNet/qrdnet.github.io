QRDNet Certified Member

This level certifies basic knowledge of the QRDNet network, community protocols, and the fundamentals of Open Science.

Examination Criteria:

  • Complete a basic quantum information science and digital security test.
  • Participate in the community channels.
  • Accept the QRDNet code of ethics.
